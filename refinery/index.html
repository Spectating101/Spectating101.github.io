Capabilities are cheap to generate. Evidence is not.

Refinery is an evidence-grounded capability commons. It separates the behavior being sought, the exact realization that provides it, the evidence attached to that realization, and the authority to recommend or reuse it.

Invariant: evidence about subject X does not silently become evidence about related subject Y.

What has actually been demonstrated

Capability ≠ implementation

One semantic capability can have multiple pinned Git, OCI, or other concrete realizations without collapsing their evidence together.

Anti-laundering boundary

A real design defect was found where project-level provenance could contaminate sibling implementations. The evidence model now requires exact subject binding.

Heterogeneous fit

LangGraph, tldraw, and uv were represented without changing the Commons kernel schema.

Cross-domain fit

The same capability → implementation → claim grammar survived a real conservation/institutional case without inventing a universal institution object.

Citation Engine bridge

Compiled Commons objects can be consumed by a separate neutral evidence/authority substrate without folding the projects together.

Negative controls survive

Refinery retains adverse results instead of forcing a reuse or commercialization win.

What is deliberately still unproved

Cold reviewer comprehension

No outside reviewer packet is counted until an actual reviewer completes the supplied protocol.

Outside use / outcome

A running interface is not adoption. A real outside event and scoped verdict/outcome must be recorded.

Authorized economic comparison

The stronger reuse-vs-rebuild test requires genuine owner/supplier authorization; public availability is not permission.

Product-market fit

No evidence maturity state, benchmark, or attestation is allowed to manufacture market value.
